---
name: Storefront truthfulness
description: Product-content limits for the Maroc Sélénite storefront.
---

The storefront must not invent verified product provenance, certifications, health benefits, testimonials, reviews, awards, delivery guarantees, real inventory, shipping rates, payment methods, or contact details. Any demo products, prices, and generated images must be clearly identified as illustrative until the business confirms them.

**Why:** The project PRD explicitly prohibits unverified commerce claims and unsupported operational promises.

**How to apply:** Keep preview/demo labeling visible on customer-facing pages. Before enabling real checkout or launch SEO, replace illustrative catalog and policy content with owner-approved facts and configured commerce services.
